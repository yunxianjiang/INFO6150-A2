Welcome

The Vallejo John F Kennedy Library and its companion branch Springstowne Library are central parts of Vallejo’s public library system, serving the community as welcoming places for learning, reading, and connection. Operated as part of the Solano County Library network, these libraries offer free access to a wide range of books, digital resources, and educational programs for all ages.
